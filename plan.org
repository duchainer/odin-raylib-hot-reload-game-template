#+title: Plan

* Train Group Survival game : Text based adventure
trijam 333-part1
Train text game

You always go forward, trying to survive longer, picking up people to keep your food, morale, fuel and water in check.

Dropping people off costs morale, but allows to swap with new more useful people.

Not picking someone up is a lesser cost.

And when you run out of one thing, the other things are even more drained, or important.

Randomness give different runs, and order of events
You can always skip past both choices, but you can't go back.
